Polkadot now keeps an open dataset of the planet’s air

Since April, home sensors in Cyprus, Koh Phangan, Réunion and 20+ countries have written more than 19 million air readings through Robonomics. Since 28 September the record lives on Polkadot. Not a roadmap promise: an archive anyone can open today, and a use of a blockchain whose main result is not financial.

The last part of the series: the Altruist sensors that sit on balconies and desks, how their firmware is built and delivered, how every unit is tested before it ships, and what people see on sensors.social and in the new Android app.

Robonomics 5.0, part two: one signed envelope, from the sensor to the chain

The connectivity layer of Robonomics 5.0: a protobuf envelope the sensor signs once and nobody re-encodes, a message format squeezed to fit a LoRa packet, and a cloud pipeline that writes compressed batches of signed readings straight into the CPS pallet.
