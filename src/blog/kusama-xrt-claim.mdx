We want to take Robonomics into production with its live community, people who still care about the project. Many Kusama XRT were handed out years ago, for crowdloans, in early test networks, in the alpha days, and now sit in wallets nobody uses. The Polkadot launch gives us a chance to start without ghost tokens.

So instead of moving Kusama balances automatically, we're opening a claim. In early December a claim page opens on Polkadot Asset Hub.

<RbImage zoom src="/blog/images/kusama-xrt-claim/timeline.png" alt="How the Kusama XRT claim works: final block on 28 September 2026, claim opens in early December on Polkadot Asset Hub, +20% bonus, 12-month claim window" />

## How it works

- Your balance is taken at the final block of the Robonomics parachain on Kusama, #12,268,835, 28 September 2026.
- You claim it with a 20% bonus.
- Open the claim page, connect the wallet that held XRT on Kusama, and claim.
- The claim is open for 12 months. After that, tokens can still be claimed, but only through an OpenGov referendum.

If you held XRT on Kusama and you're still with us, claim your tokens in early December. The link will be on the [tokenomics page](/tokenomics/) and in [XRT Traders](https://t.me/xrt_trade).
