A donation is voluntary

There is no minimum amount and no obligation to donate. A donation is not payment for goods or services: it gives no tokens, rights, priority support or claim on our time. For paid work, please use the contact form.

Conversion

Received funds may be exchanged into JPYC (a yen stablecoin) or Japanese yen (JPY) and are then used by the company for the purposes listed above.

Refunds

A refund is possible on a direct request to ceo@akagi.dev within 30 days of the transfer date. Please include the transaction hash and the address the funds should be returned to.
